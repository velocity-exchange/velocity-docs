# What is unsettled P&L?

### Settling P&L

Settling P&L refers to settling the total funds available in the per-market **P&L Pool**. See [Introduction to Profit & Loss](/protocol/trading/profit-loss#types-of-pl) for how Unrealised, Settled, and Claimable/Unsettled P&L relate, and [Accounting & Settlement](/protocol/trading/profit-loss/accounting-settlement) for how the P&L Pool is funded.

In short: for each dollar withdrawn from realised profits, there must be a corresponding dollar of realised losses sitting in the P&L Pool. If a profitable position's realised P&L exceeds what's currently settled in the pool, the excess is **unsettled P&L** until the pool is replenished by other users' settled losses. See [P&L Pool](/protocol/how-it-works/profit-loss-pool) for what happens when there isn't enough Settled P&L.

### Margining of Unsettled P&L  

If positive P&L is unsettled, it may account for less initial margin for trading perpetuals than USDT. Please see the treatment of non-USD assets here [Cross-Collateral Deposits](/protocol/getting-started/cross-collateral-deposits).

Each market has a maximum imbalanced P&L limit: a cap on how much net positive P&L (winners minus losers) the market's pool is expected to safely cover. If the net user P&L in a market breaches this limit, the asset weight used to value that P&L as collateral is decreased. If a shortfall still occurs at settlement, the excess above the limit is covered by the Insurance Fund, up to the market's own max insurance fund limit.

For example, say the BTC-PERP market has a maximum imbalanced P&L limit of \$5,000,000 and a max Insurance Fund limit of \$1,000,000 for the market. After a sustained rally, BTC-PERP users are collectively up \$6,000,000 of net unsettled P&L, \$1,000,000 over the limit:

- Because net P&L has breached the \$5,000,000 limit, the asset weight applied to unsettled BTC-PERP P&L is reduced, so it counts for less initial margin toward opening or increasing positions elsewhere.
- If traders then try to settle or withdraw that P&L and the P&L Pool can't cover the \$1,000,000 excess, the Insurance Fund pays out the shortfall, up to its own \$1,000,000 cap for that market. Any deficit beyond that cap remains unsettled until the pool is replenished by other users' losses.

<Callout type="important">
Discounting the unsettled P&L lowers the maximum leverage extended for users (and only decreases a user's initial margin).  
</Callout>

These guards don't lower the maintenance margin weights and are not relevant for liquidations.

### Withdrawals

Only the lesser value of your Free Collateral and Asset Balance will be available for withdrawal (without opening a borrow). Realising positive unrealised P&L (by reducing/closing positions) will allow for that P&L to be settled to your Asset Balance for withdrawal.

<Callout type="important">
A suggestion for users that want to stay in their position and withdraw unrealised profits in excess of their asset balance is to reduce/close then reopen the position.  
</Callout>
