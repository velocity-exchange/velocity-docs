# Maker Fee Rebate

_For more information on our fee tiers, please see [trading fees](/protocol/trading/trading-fees)_

The below fee schedule details the rebates provided to Market Makers that have their orders filled by other users on the platform.  

In order to ensure an order is a maker order, it will need the 'post only' flag. Otherwise even resting orders can match as a taker.

The current incentive program will be subject to change. All changes will be reflected in this schedule.  

| **Maker order matched against** | **Maker Fee (flat, regardless of Taker Fee)** |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| A user's taker order             | -.25bps                                          |

The -0.25 bps rebate is uniform across all populated fee tiers. It is scaled only by the market's own `fee_adjustment` parameter, so it can be higher or lower on a specific market.

<Callout type="info">
When a taker fills against the vAMM instead of a resting user order, there is no user maker to pay a rebate to. The vAMM itself only earns a rebate if the `VammMakerRebate` feature flag is enabled (off by default), and in that case the amount is folded into the AMM's own fee accounting rather than paid out to any user.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
Velocity does not offer a fee discount for staking a governance token. The legacy gov-token stake fee discount was removed. Maker rebates depend only on the fee tier shown above.
</Callout>
