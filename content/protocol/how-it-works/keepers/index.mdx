# Keepers

## Keeper Bots

Keeper Bots listen, store, sort and fill valid limit orders. Keepers do this by compiling all valid open orders found on-chain and organising them into an off-chain orderbook. These orders are sorted by price, and ties on price are broken by age (arrival order), oldest first.

Each Keeper holds its own orderbook (hence _decentralised_ orderbook).

Keepers then listen to trigger conditions and match crossing orders and limit orders against the AMM when the users’ trigger or limit price is met.

<Callout type="info">
Keeper and DLOB fills apply to **perpetual** markets only. Velocity has no spot order book, so there are no spot orders for keepers to fill. Spot markets exist only for collateral and [Borrow & Lend](/protocol/borrow-lend).
</Callout>

For performing this critical duty, the Keepers earn a fee for every trade they execute. Similar to liquidation bots, Keepers will compete for fees in a decentralised system; the most profitable Keepers are:

- **Fastest**: Speed is a primary advantage in competing for fees.
- **Best price improvement for takers**: Keepers that provide better prices to takers gain a competitive edge.
- **Fulfil orders in the protocol’s desired sequence**, Keepers that execute the oldest order first are preferred. Filler rewards also cap out at 10% of the taker fee, which favors filling larger orders within that limit.

Reference implementations for the filler and trigger Keepers are provided: [Keeper Bots](/developers/trading-automation).
